## Two-audience principle

The technical report and the executive brief are **different products, not the same product at
two lengths**. Compressing the technical report does not yield the brief; the brief answers
different questions — what happened, why it matters commercially, what we know, what we don't,
and what to do. Compare the two EU-FIN-001 documents directly: the executive version explains
adversary-in-the-middle without using the term, and leads with the organisation's own
configuration failure.
